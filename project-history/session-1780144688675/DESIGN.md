# Design System for Dangote Energy Logistics

This document outlines the core visual principles and tokens for the Dangote Energy Logistics application, embodying the brand values of Heritage, Trust, Robustness, and Scale.

## Brand Essence & Visual Tone

Our visual identity conveys:
*   **Trustworthiness**: Through consistent, predictable layouts and robust typography.
*   **Authority**: Via a professional color palette and clear information hierarchy.
*   **Efficiency**: Clean lines, ample white space, and purposeful interactions.
*   **African Heritage**: Subtle nods through color accents and high-quality, relevant imagery.

## Color Palette

The chosen palette reflects professionalism and the energy sector's core.
*   **Primary (Brand Blue)**: `#0A2463` - A deep, strong blue signifying trust, stability, and corporate authority. This will be the dominant color for backgrounds, primary calls to action, and key navigational elements.
*   **Accent (Brand Gold)**: `#DAA520` - A rich, warm gold representing energy, value, quality, and the vibrancy of African markets. Used for highlights, active states, important data points, and secondary calls to action.
*   **Neutrals**: A range of dark charcoals, grays, and off-whites for text, backgrounds, and borders, ensuring high readability and a clean aesthetic.
    *   `#212121` (Text Primary): For main headings and body text.
    *   `#616161` (Text Secondary): For supporting text, labels, and less prominent information.
    *   `#F5F5F5` (Background Light): For general content areas and card backgrounds.
    *   `#1C1C1C` (Background Dark): For specific UI sections or as a dark mode base.
*   **System Colors**: Standard green, yellow, and red for success, warning, and error messages, ensuring immediate comprehension.

## Typography

Readability and clear hierarchy are paramount.
*   **Headings**: **Montserrat** (sans-serif) - Used for all headings (H1-H6) and prominent display text. Its strong, modern character conveys confidence and clarity. Font weights will vary from Semibold to Bold to define hierarchy.
*   **Body Text**: **Inter** (sans-serif) - Used for all paragraph text, labels, and UI elements. Inter is highly legible at various sizes, optimized for screens, and contributes to the system's functional and reliable feel. Font weights will be Regular and Medium.
*   **Font Sizes**: A scalable font sizing system (based on `rem`) derived from a 4-point scale, ensuring consistent vertical rhythm and accessibility.

## Spacing

A consistent 4-point grid system will be used for all internal and external spacing, ensuring visual harmony and predictability.
*   **Base Unit**: `4px`
*   **Scale**: `4px`, `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, etc.
*   This structured approach minimizes visual clutter and aids in creating professional, organized layouts.

## Border Radius

Subtle rounding will be applied to UI elements to soften edges without compromising professionalism.
*   **Minimal Rounding**: Small `4px` or `8px` radii for buttons, input fields, and cards.
*   **No Excessive Rounding**: Elements should retain a robust, architectural feel; full `pill` shapes will be reserved for specific small components or tags.

## Shadows

Subtle, diffused shadows will be used sparingly to indicate elevation and interaction.
*   **Light Elevation**: `0px 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` for default cards and actionable elements.
*   **Interactive States**: Slightly deeper shadows for hover states or active elements, drawing attention without being distracting.

## Component Consistency

All interactive components (buttons, inputs, cards, tables, navigation elements) will adhere to these tokens, ensuring a unified user experience across the application. Hover states, focus states, and disabled states will be clearly defined and consistent.

## Responsive Behavior

The design system will be inherently responsive, adapting layouts and content gracefully across various screen sizes from mobile to large desktop displays, ensuring a seamless experience for users across different devices.

## AI Slop Detection Avoidance

We actively avoid generic AI-generated design patterns:
*   No gratuitous gradients on every element.
*   Avoid default purple-to-blue palettes.
*   No "glass morphism" effects without clear functional purpose.
*   Rounded corners will be intentional and not applied haphazardly.
*   Animations will be minimal and purposeful, enhancing usability rather than distracting.
*   Hero sections will be bespoke, utilizing brand imagery and colors instead of generic stock gradients.
*   Typography will have character, moving beyond generic sans-serif stacks.

By adhering to these principles and tokens, the Dangote Energy Logistics application will project the strength, reliability, and trusted heritage of its inspiration.
